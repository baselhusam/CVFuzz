The scarcity rule

Signal Lime is reserved for one thing per view: the boundary, the active item, or the primary action. If two lime elements compete on screen, one of them is wrong. Everything else is neutral — Ink, Slate, Steel.

Lime inverts, not survives

On light surfaces Signal Lime fails contrast. Primary actions become Ink; the accent survives only as a 4px marker, underline or fill behind Ink text.

States darken by ~20%

Failed #F45B69 → #C8323F, Stable #5FD08A → #2E8F5B, Degraded #F0A63C → #B4741A. Tints use the same hue at 8%.

Frames stay dark, always

Video, filmstrips and detection canvases keep the Ink field in both themes. A light chrome around a dark viewport is correct, not a bug.

11

Rules & accessibility

The identity survives through restraint. These are the decisions that keep the product feeling like CVFuzz rather than a generic dark dashboard.

Do
01State the parameter, the threshold and the evidence together. "σ 0.38, mAP 0.52, 768 frames."
02Keep one lime element per view — the boundary or the primary action.
03Use mono for every number, id, path and axis label so columns align.
04Show the acceptance line on every chart, even when nothing failed.
05Report calmly. A failure is a finding, not an incident.
Don't
01Fill large areas with Signal Lime, or set lime text below 16px on light surfaces.
02Add glitch, scanlines or noise as decoration. Distortion is data here, not texture.
03Use colour alone for status, or a bare arrow for a delta.
04Round a metric to hide uncertainty, or show a number without its baseline.
05Retype the wordmark or place the logo more than once per screen.
Contrast
Paper on Ink17.9:1 AAA
Ink on Signal16.2:1 AAA
Steel on Ink7.8:1 AA
Slate on Ink4.3:1 · 14px+
Signal on White1.2:1 FAIL

Slate is for 14px and above only. Below that, step up to Steel.

Interaction
Focus is never removed2px lime ring, 2px offset, on every interactive element including table rows and frame thumbnails.
Hit targets ≥ 32pxCompact density shrinks padding, never the clickable box. Icon buttons stay 32×32.
Video is keyboard-drivenSpace toggles, ←/→ step one frame, shift+← jumps to the previous failure marker.
Reduced motion honouredPulse, shimmer and scan animations resolve to a static state under prefers-reduced-motion.
Copy
Write
"Detection lost on 768 of 2,400 frames at σ 0.38."
Not
"Critical failure! Your model is unsafe!"
Write
"No instability found within the tested range."
Not
"All good — your model is robust!"
