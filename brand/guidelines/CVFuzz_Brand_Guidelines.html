03 / ColorCVFuzz / Brand Guidelines / 08

Proportion and contrast

Use color with intent.

Brand color guides attention. It must never become the only carrier of state or meaning.

70% Field
20% Structure
10% Signal

Recommended visual proportion - not a rigid layout formula.

AaInk on Paper - 18.22:1 - AAA
AaInk on Signal - 16.18:1 - AAA
AaSignal on White - 1.20:1 - Fail

Never set small lime text on white. Pair state colors with labels, shapes, patterns, or icons.
